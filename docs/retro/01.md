# Retro 1

Date: 2026-10-12. Scope: the session that asked "how do I continue outstanding work?" and then brought the tickets in `.scratch/fuda-writes-build/issues/` up to date.

## Findings

1. **Tickets went stale when work shipped.** Eight tickets (`01`, `04`, `11`, `12`, `14`, `19`, `20`, `22`) were built but still showed `ready-for-agent` with open boxes. `docs/agents/issue-tracker.md` said where the `Status:` line lives, not who updates it.
2. **No check on `Status:` values.** Values were mixed: `done`, `resolved`, and free text such as `in-progress: ...` (tickets `09`, `10`). Ticket `05`, a build ticket, said `resolved`.
3. **`@personal-agent.md` in `CLAUDE.md` points at a file that does not exist in this checkout.** Harmless: other developers may have it.
4. **macOS `sed` has no `\|` for "or".** A filter silently ticked three by-hand boxes. Caught and undone.

## Changes made

- `docs/agents/issue-tracker.md`: added the rule that the commit that finishes a ticket ticks its boxes and sets `Status:`. A box only a person can check stays open, and the ticket becomes `ready-for-human`.
- `api/tickets_test.go`: new test, run by `make check-api`. Every ticket under `.scratch/*/issues/` needs a `Status:` line with one of: `needs-triage`, `needs-info`, `ready-for-agent`, `ready-for-human`, `wontfix`, `done`, `claimed`, `resolved`.
- Tickets `09` and `10`: status `in-progress: ...` became `ready-for-human`, with the old text kept as "Left to do by hand: ...".
- Ticket `05`: `resolved` became `done`.
- Ticket statuses brought in line with the code: `01`, `11`, `12`, `14`, `20` are `done`; `04`, `19`, `22` are `ready-for-human`. Ticket `14` lost its Windows by-hand box, because Windows is not tested at this stage.

## Vocabulary

- `resolved`: a decision ticket (wayfinder) with its question answered. Lives in `.scratch/fuda-writes/issues/`.
- `done`: a build ticket whose work shipped. Not one of the five triage labels; this repo's own value.
- `claimed`: a wayfinder ticket someone is working on.
